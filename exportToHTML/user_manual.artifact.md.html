## 1. Dashboard & Navigation 
 
When you open **pobvol Open Checklists**, you arrive at the **Dashboard (Start Screen)**. 
 
> [!NOTE] 
> The top app bar displays **pobvol Open Checklists** along with a **Settings** gear icon in the top right corner. 
 
The Dashboard features two main card options: 
- **Checklists**: View total stored checklists and tap to navigate to the **Checklists Overview**. 
- **Submissions**: View recorded submissions count and tap to navigate to the **Submissions Overview**. 
 
--- 
 
## 2. Managing Checklists 
 
Navigate to the **Checklists Overview** by tapping the **Checklists** card on the Dashboard or using the back navigation. 
 
### Creating a Checklist 
1. Tap the floating **`+`** button in the bottom-right corner. 
2. Enter the **Title** (required), **Language**, **Description**, and **Category**. 
3. Select an existing category or enter a custom category. 
4. Tap **Add** to save the checklist to the database. 
 
> [!TIP] 
> Custom categories entered when creating or editing a checklist are automatically saved to the database categories table for future use. 
 
### Searching & Filtering Checklists 
- **Search Bar**: Tap the search icon (🔍) in the top bar to search checklists by title, description, or category. 
- **Category Filter Chips**: Filter the list by tapping any category chip at the top (`All`, `UVV`, `Health`, etc.). 
- **Add Category Button**: Tap **`+ Category`** directly in the filter bar to add new categories dynamically. 
 
### Checklist Card Actions 
On each checklist card, you can tap: 
- **Card Body / Card Title**: Opens the **Submissions Overview** listing only submissions assigned to that specific checklist. 
- 📋 **Fill Icon**: Opens the form to fill out and record new checklist answers. 
- 💬 **Questions Icon**: Opens the **Question Management Screen** to add, edit, or reorder questions. 
- ✏️ **Edit Icon**: Edits the checklist title, category, description, and language. 
- 🗑️ **Delete Icon**: Confirms deletion of the checklist. Deleting a checklist automatically deletes all associated questions and submissions via database cascade.

## 3. Managing Questions & Reordering 
 
Tap the **Questions** (💬) icon on any checklist card to manage its questions. 
 
### Adding & Editing Questions 
1. Tap the floating **`+`** button. 
2. Enter the **Question Title** (required) and optional **Description**. 
3. Choose an **Answer Type** from the dropdown (`text`, `number`, `checkbox`, `combobox`, `nfc`, `qr-code`). 
4. For `combobox`, enter comma-separated options (e.g. `Morning, Evening, Night`). 
5. Toggle whether the question is **Required** or **Optional**. 
6. Optionally enter an explicit **Sort Order** number. 
7. Tap **Save**. 
 
### Changing Question Sort Order 
- Use the **Up (↑)** and **Down (↓)** arrow buttons on each question card to reorder questions up or down. 
- Each question card displays a sort badge (e.g., `#1`, `#2`) showing its exact position. 
- Up/Down reordering automatically updates and saves the new `sortno` positions in the SQLite database.

## 4. Supported Answer Types 
 
| Answer Type | Description | Input / Interaction Method | 
| :--- | :--- | :--- | 
| **`text`** | Text Response | Multi-line text field for entering comments or descriptions. | 
| **`number`** | Numeric Input | Single-line numeric input field. | 
| **`checkbox`** | Yes / No Selection | Toggle checkbox (`Yes / Checked` or `No / Unchecked`). | 
| **`combobox`** | Single Choice | Radio button selection from custom options. | 
| **`nfc`** | NFC Scan | Tap physical NFC tags against the device to automatically read and populate NDEF text messages or Tag UIDs into a multi-line text field. | 
| **`qr-code`** | QR Code Scan | Tap **Scan QR Code** to open the live camera scanner (CameraX + ML Kit) to scan QR codes or enter/simulate code content into a multi-line field. |

## 5. Filling Out a Checklist & Submitting Answers 
 
To perform a check or inspection: 
1. Tap the 📋 **Fill Icon** on a checklist card. 
2. **Submission Details**: 
   - **Inspector / User Name**: Auto-filled from settings or editable. 
   - **Check Date**: Prefilled with today's date (`dd.MM.yyyy`) and fully editable. 
   - **Notes**: Optional general notes or comments for the check. 
3. **Answering Questions**: 
   - Answer each question according to its type. 
   - For **`nfc`** questions: Hold an NFC tag against the back of your device to scan, or tap **Scan NFC Tag**. 
   - For **`qr-code`** questions: Tap **Scan QR Code** to open the camera scanner and capture the code. 
4. Tap **Submit Checklist Answers**. Validation guarantees all required questions are answered before submitting.

## 6. Submissions Overview & Filtering 
 
Access recorded checks from the Dashboard or by tapping a checklist card. 
 
### Submissions Features 
- **Checklist Filtering**: When opened from a specific checklist card, the Submissions Overview displays only submissions for that checklist (with a clearable `Filter: <Title>` chip). 
- **Status Filter Chips**: Tap `All`, `COMPLETED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, or `PENDING` chips to filter by check status. 
- **Search**: Search submissions by inspector, notes, date, or checklist title. 
- **Submission Cards**: Displays Checklist Title, Status chip, Inspector, Notes, Check Date (`dd.MM.yyyy`), and recorded Timestamp. 
 
--- 
 
## 7. Editing Submissions & PDF Export 
 
Tap ✏️ **Edit** or 📄 **PDF Report** on any submission card. 
 
### PDF Report Export 
1. Tap **PDF Report** on a submission card or in the **Edit Submission** screen top bar / bottom action bar. 
2. The app generates a formatted A4 PDF document containing: 
   - Header with App Title and Document Title. 
   - Inspection details (Checklist Title, Check Date, Inspector, Status, Notes). 
   - Question & Answer results table. 
   - Generation date and timestamp footer. 
3. Opens the PDF immediately in your Android device's PDF viewer or allows sharing.

## 8. App Settings & Category Management 
 
Tap the ⚙️ **Settings** gear icon in the top app bar to access **General Settings**. 
 
- **Preferred Screen Mode**: Choose between `System`, `Light`, or `Dark` theme modes. 
- **Preferred Language**: Select default language. 
- **User Name**: Set the default inspector name used when filling out checklists. 
- **Manage Categories**: 
  - View all stored database categories from the `categories` table. 
  - Enter a name and tap **Add** to save a new category. 
  - Tap the 🗑️ **Delete** icon next to any category to remove it from the database. 
 
--- 
 
> [!IMPORTANT] 
> **Data Safety & Backup**: All checklists, questions, submissions, and categories are securely stored in your local Android SQLite database with foreign key cascades and automatic schema migrations.
